ABOUT QQTea

Your friendly & affordable neighborhood bubble tea store.

Born In Autumn

QQTea started with a simple idea: bubble tea should be fun, customizable, and affordable enough to enjoy more often.

Inspired by autumn flavours and the comfort of a familiar drink, we created QQTea as a fictional online tea shop built around simple ordering and convenient pickup.

Made Your Way

Choose your drink, adjust the sweetness and ice, and select a pickup time that works for you.

Our goal is to make getting your favourite drink easy to come back to.
